I've been challenging myself to see how many points I can score in as little turns as possible - I'm not sure how to score the differences in both but for now I'll say that the score of this challenge is:
Challenge Score = Points in Solo Game $\div$ Turns Played

Rules:

- Maximum of 2 events/projects/ways/traits/landmarks
- Choose any cards to be in your action supply piles (including VP cards) and permitting deprecated cards
- Reset the game as many times as you need to get the correct draw. For example, I need the 3/4 money split to start without the overgrown estate in the second hand. I also need to hit $5 before the second shuffle.

I would love to hear what score any of you can get and how you got there. I'll share my approach and final score below:

### Score:

_699 Points, 8 Turns_ - **87.4**

### Setup:

##### Action Cards (5):

Inventor, Squire, King's Court, Council Room, Courier

##### VP Cards (5):

Marchlands, Silk Roads, Gardens, Duke, Castles

##### Projects (2):

Academy, Cathedral

### Method

##### Turn 1 ($3)

Buy access to Cathedral (a project) so that trashing can start next turn.

I think that if someone were to improve my strategy it would involve skipping the trashing and hoping for a nearly perfect draw on turn 7 or 8.
I daresay that aside from optimizing these first two turns, changing a project to a trait or event, or getting lucky and shaving off a turn there is no way to improve upon the score I've achieved.

##### Turn 2 ($4):

Trash a non-overgrown estate shelter (Cathedral) and buy a Courier. I need this Courier to get 5$ in my next hand without slowing down my engine building.

##### Turn 3 (3 Coppers, shelter, Courier):

Trash the shelter (Cathedral). Use Courier to play a Copper, which makes $5 to buy the Academy Project. I will not get 5$ again until turn 8!

I now have just 9 cards in my entire deck so I will shuffle this turn to draw again, which means I can use my Courier well later.

##### Turn 4 (4 Coppers, NO Courier):

Trash any card. Buy an Inventor. I still have just 9 cards so I shuffle again.

##### Turn 5 (Inventor, Courier, 2+ Copper):

Trash any non-action card in my hand.
Use Inventor to gain an Inventor. By now I already have a villager from Academy + buying an Inventor, and I just got a second villager. Spend one villager to play Courier to play the Inventor I just gained. Use the Inventor to gain a Council Room. Council Rooms now cost just 3$ and so I buy one more.

I now have 3 villagers via Academy and am too overwhelmed thinking about how many I am about to have. Please forgive me for not mentioning them, I think you will understand why shortly.

##### Turn 6:

The key here is to spend villagers to use both Council Rooms to draw every card. Play one Council Room (which must appear in the first 5), then play an Inventor to gain another Inventor. Play Council Room again to draw every card. Play your second Inventor to gain another Inventor, then use Courier to play that Inventor. Then use the last Inventor to gain a King's Court. Then use your leftover $6 and 3 buys to get 2 King's Courts and an Inventor.

##### Turn 7 (King's Court, King's Court, Council Room, Copper):

King's Court -> King's Court.
King's Court -> Council Room
King's Court -> King's Court
King's Court -> Inventor to gain (Inventor x3)
King's Court -> Inventor to gain (King's Court x3)
King's Court -> Council Room
King's Court -> King's Court -> King's Court...
Repeat until you've gained, drawn, and used every King's Court, then use King's Court on Inventor to gain every Squire. Then use King's Court on Council Room to Draw all of the new cards and Throne them as many times as possible to get +6 buys for each. Every King's Court should have been used and every squire has been used. Then use every leftover Inventor to gain victory cards.

At least 2 dozen villagers remain and about 70 buys remain. Don't discard for money with Marchlands because everything is free at this point and the VP cards in hand count a point each for Grand Castle. You should be able to empty nearly every action supply pile and VP pile in the game. If you can't, buy Couriers last to increase the value of the Gardens

##### My Run:

I didn't manage to do the 7 turn process I described earlier because of some good-but-not-perfect luck but the method I described was done roughly the same way in 8 turns and I've included screenshots. If I had gained 1 VP card before the buy phase I would have got to 700 points.
